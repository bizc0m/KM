## Points cles

- Geneva AI Summit 2027 will gather governments, international organizations, science, business and civil society in Geneva.
- The working motto is centered on bridging innovation and trust.
- The Swiss Confederation estimates organization costs at almost CHF 6 million.
- Sponsorship contributions should not influence the strategic orientation, official agenda or political outcomes.
- Fabiola Gianotti, former CERN Director-General, has been appointed as delegate to implement the summit objectives.
